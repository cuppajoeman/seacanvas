About Seacanvas

There’s something magical about the place where sea meets land and sky. Something that draws us back to the water’s edge over and over.

In Nova Scotia we have the highest tides in the world, and the movement of Fundy tides over vast mudflats creates an endlessly changing and dramatic seascape. On the Atlantic shore, there are miles of sand and seaweed-covered rocks stretching out to vast ocean, a minimalist colour palette of gold, granite grey on blue.

These oil canvases attempt to capture some of the feel of Nova Scotia seascapes, for when we aren’t there in person or when we want to be reminded of what lies around the corner.

Beverley Cassidy
